# Encore product spec

Encore is a tab inside the Zara app. It has one loop: **bring it back → swipe → climb → unlock**. Everything below is a rule the app must enforce or display.

## 1. The three currencies

| Thing | What it does | How you get it | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Archive Key** (gold) | Gets you into Archive Drops | Bringing a Zara garment back to an Encore Bin | Bought, sold, transferred, expired, used as a discount |
| **Circular Score** (lime, "points") | Ranks you in your weekly city league | Circular actions (table in §3) | Earned by spending |
| **Tier** (Thread → Archive) | Gives perks: early entry, bonus keys, battle entry | Finishing high in your weekly league | Bought |

## 2. Earning keys (Phase 1: Encore Bins)

Flow: **Scan → Confirm → Drop → Key lands → Follow the lot** (screens 1.1-1.5).

1. **Scan** the care label in Store Mode. The reference code (and the NaviLens code on new labels) identifies the garment. Any Zara piece, any condition, no receipt.
2. **Confirm** the garment (photo, name, ref, composition from the catalogue lookup Pre-Owned already uses). User rates condition honestly: *Like new / Loved / Worn out*. This only affects the grade-A bonus. A map shows the bin by the returns counter.
3. **Drop** it in the bin. The bin weighs it, its UHF reader confirms the RFID label on recent pieces (~0.9 s), and the scan matches the reference. Older pieces are confirmed by scan plus weight.
4. **Key lands:** +1 Archive Key and +50 Circular Score immediately. A lot number is assigned. A second key arrives if the hub grades the piece A.
5. **Lot tracker:** Collected → Graded at the hub → one destination: *Vintage lane*, *Re-cut edition* or *Fiber*. When it sells, the original owner is told ("your trench found its encore"; buyer shown by first name only, if they opt in).

Rules:
- 1 key per Zara garment, 2 if graded A. **Cap: 10 keys per calendar month**, shown plainly in the wallet.
- Non-Zara garments are accepted for Cáritas, with no keys.
- Condition spot checks stop gaming.
- Keys need GDPR opt-in at first scan.

## 3. Circular Score (points)

| Action | Points | Cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bring a garment back (bin scan) | +50 (plus 1 key, 2 if grade A) | 10 keys / month | Only way to earn keys |
| Post a look in secondhand or re-cut | +15, and +5 per 10 hearts | 50 per post | Photos opt-in and moderated |
| Add a fit note to a piece you own | +5 | 25 / week | true to size / runs small / runs big + tags |
| Log a repair | +30 | none | |
| Sell a piece on Zara Pre-Owned | +40 | none | The "micro-entrepreneur" path |
| Finish the daily deck (20 swipes) | +5 | 35 / week | No streaks |
| Win a Style Battle | +20 | | Plus a spot on the store screens |
| Buy anything | **0** | | Status is never bought |

## 4. City Leagues and tiers

- Leagues of **50** people in your declared city, matched by join date. Week runs Monday-Sunday; **resets every Sunday**.
- **Top 10 move up a tier, bottom 10 move down.** Seasons follow collections (SS, AW) and end with **Encore Wrapped**.

| Tier | How you get there | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Thread | Join and do one circular action | The daily deck and the city Looks feed |
| Stitch | Finish a week in the top 20 of 50 | Fit notes from owners in your kept size on vintage listings |
| Seam | Top 10 of your Stitch league, two weeks running | **Skip-the-line pass:** enter every drop 10 minutes early (09:50) |
| Cut | Top 10 of Seam **and** 2 garments returned this season | +1 bonus key a month; Style Battle entry |
| Archive | Top 5% of the city at season end | +2 keys a season, Archive Insider badge, the city Encore night, first look at re-cut editions |

- A skip-the-line pass can also be earned by winning a Style Battle. One pass per drop; unused passes carry over.
- Leaderboards turn off in one tap; keys and badges stay.

## 5. Archive Drops (Phase 3)

Every **Tuesday at 10:00**, in-app, quantity-limited. Monday shows a teaser (lanes, counts, pieces you hyped). One reminder at 09:45, never more.

| Lane | Keys | What it is | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vintage by era | 1 | Graded, cleaned pre-owned Zara grouped by trend and year, numbered ("Zara 2012 · Indie Vanguard · No. 0417 of 2,400"), Verified tag | Grade A: original price. Grade B: 70% of original |
| Re-cut editions | 5 | Hand re-cut by partner ateliers (Arteixo cluster) from garments graded unwearable; numbered editions of 20-200 | Atelier prices |
| First access | 3 | Most-wanted new-collection pieces, 24 h before the public | Same price as in store |

Rules:
- **The key opens the door; money buys the piece.** Buying spends the keys and charges the price.
- Quantities and remaining stock are stated ("1,612 still available"). No countdown, no restock, no markdowns.
- Piece detail shows: number in run, grade, era edit, fit notes from owners in your size, and its story in facts (where and when it was brought back, graded and listed).
- After purchase: certificate card on the profile; pick up in store or ship with the Tuesday delivery; free returns to the bin (with a key).
- Buying earns 0 points.

## 6. Daily deck (Style Swipe)

- 20 cards a day: this week's drop, upcoming limited pieces, graded vintage, re-cut editions, new arrivals.
- Hype (♥) or pass (✕). Hyped pieces get "first word" on Tuesday. Passing shows fewer pieces like it.
- If you own a piece in the deck, you're offered a fit note.
- Finishing gives +5 and a taste profile ("62% Indie Vanguard · 28% Utility and denim").
- No streaks: skipping a day loses nothing.

## 7. Community (the Depop layer)

- **Looks feed:** city feed of people wearing secondhand and re-cut Zara, tagged with lot and pieces. Filters: City, Following, Re-cut, plus a Battle chip and a city-goal chip.
- **Post a look:** photo, tag Encore pieces from your lots and drops, one line, choose audience (Madrid feed / friends only / enter next battle). +15.
- **Closet page** (every user): avatar, handle, tier, followers, garments back, hearts this week, aesthetic tags, tabs *Her closet / Looks / Fit notes*. Closet items show where they are now (in Tuesday's drop, for sale on Pre-Owned with price). Actions: Follow, Ask about fit, Shop their edit. "Encore Curator" badge for high-taste users: status from taste and returns, never spending.
- **Style Battle:** two looks, city votes, one vote per battle, live split bar, open all day (no countdown). Winner +20 and store screens. Entering needs the Cut tier; voting is open to all.

## 8. City goal (community-wide)

Every garment anyone in the city brings back counts toward one season goal (demo: Madrid, 61,240 of 100,000).

| Milestone | Zara does |
|---|---|
| 25,000 | Free repair weekend in every Zara in the city |
| 50,000 | 5,000 paid training hours at Cáritas Moda re- |
| 75,000 | The city votes on a re-cut edition; proceeds to Cáritas |
| 100,000 | 10,000 trees planted with the city's parks department, plus the city Encore night |

- Show the user's own contribution ("7 of them yours"), and a **city race** by share of goal (Madrid 61%, Barcelona 54%, London 48%: illustrative).
- Rewards are stated as actions with numbers, never as offsets or "carbon neutral".

## 9. Keys wallet and profile

- **Wallet:** balance, earned this season, used, cap progress (x of 10 this month), history of every +/− with the garment or drop behind it, rule chips.
- **Profile:** stats (garments back, kg given a second life, lots followed), badges earned only by actions (First Return, Fit Guru, Lot Follower, Battle Winner, Archive Insider), certificates of bought pieces, one-tap switches (city leaderboards, photos in feed, drop notifications).
- **Encore Wrapped** (season end): pieces back, kg, keys earned, lots followed, best weekly rank, signature era; share as image.

## 10. Onboarding and consent

One screen: choose city (declared, never tracked), photos (opt in later, off by default), confirm 16+. "Let's go" and "Not now" are equal weight. Copy: "No streaks. No countdowns. Keys never expire, so nothing is lost by waiting."

## 11. Guardrails (legal and ethical)

- No spend-based rewards, expiry or streaks (leaked EU Digital Fairness Act draft, 9 Nov 2026: right to switch off streak rewards, fines up to 6% of turnover).
- No timers, low-stock nagging or confirm-shaming (BEUC 2025 complaint against Shein; DSA cases against Shein and Temu).
- Green-claims directive (from 27 Sep 2026): factual claims only.
- 16+ only for leagues and battles; human plus AI moderation for photos.
- Cáritas keeps every reusable garment it already receives; Encore only takes grade-A Zara pieces for drops and the unwearable rest for re-cut and fiber.
