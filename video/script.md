# Video script (v3, 4:45)

Narration, in order, with the screen or card shown. Captions with timings are in Encore_App_Walkthrough_v3.srt.

- **TITLE** — This is Encore: a new tab in the Zara app. Bring your old Zara back, and earn your way into exclusive drops.
- **CARD:why** — Why it matters: about a hundred thousand Zara pieces are listed on Vinted every day, but secondhand has no status. When Zara dropped Galliano Re-form, it sold out in under ten minutes. Encore gives worn clothes that same drop energy.
- **CARD:offer** — Here's what it gives you. Every Tuesday, an Archive Drop opens with three kinds of pieces: graded Zara vintage from 2010 to today, one-off re-cut editions made from returned clothes, and early access to the next collection.
- **CARD:system** — It all runs on three things. Keys get you into the drop. Points rank you in your city. And your tier gives you perks, like skipping the line.
- **onb** — When you first open Encore, pick your city, choose whether to share photos, and confirm you're sixteen or over.
- **home** — Your home shows your keys and city rank at the top, the next drop, and four actions: bring a piece back, swipe, looks, and your league.
- **scan** — Step one: earn a key. In store, scan the care label of any Zara piece, in any condition. No receipt needed.
- **conf** — Confirm the garment, rate its condition honestly, and find the Encore Bin.
- **drop** — Drop it in. The bin weighs it and checks the label.
- **key** — You get one Archive Key, plus fifty points, and a second key if the hub grades it A.
- **keys** — Every key you earn and spend is logged in your wallet. You can earn up to ten a month, and keys never expire, have no cash value, and can't be bought.
- **lot** — Back in the app, you can follow your garment's lot, and you're told when it finds a new owner.
- **swipe** — Step two: Style Swipe. You get twenty cards a day. Tap the heart to hype a piece, and you'll get a reminder when it drops.
- **swipe2** — Pass on the rest. There are no streaks, so skipping a day costs you nothing.
- **looks** — Step three: the community. Post outfits with your Encore pieces to earn points, and see what your city is wearing.
- **closet** — Every user has a closet, like on Depop. Follow people whose style you like, ask them about fit, and shop what they brought back, or what they sell on Zara Pre-Owned.
- **closet2** — Curators get their status from taste and returns, never from spending.
- **citygoal** — And every garment anyone in Madrid brings back counts toward one city goal. Each milestone unlocks something Zara does for the city: a free repair weekend, paid training at Caritas, a re-cut edition the city votes on, and ten thousand trees.
- **battle** — In a Style Battle, two looks face off and your city votes.
- **battle2** — The winner earns twenty points, and a spot on the store screens.
- **league** — Step four: your City League. Points from returns, looks and fit notes rank you against fifty people in your city. Spending never earns points.
- **CARD:tiers** — Every week, the top ten in your league move up a tier. Thread is where everyone starts, with the daily deck and the city feed. Stitch adds fit notes from owners your size. Seam gives you the skip-the-line pass, ten minutes early. Cut adds a bonus key every month and Style Battle entry. And Archive, the top five percent of your city each season, gets bonus keys, the city Encore night, and first look at re-cut editions.
- **pass** — Reach Seam, and your skip-the-line pass shows up, ready for Tuesday.
- **dhome** — Step five: the drop. Every Tuesday, three lanes open, and each lane has its own key price.
- **CARD:pay** — Here's how buying works. A key gets you in, and money buys the piece. Vintage takes one key: grade A sells at the original price, grade B at seventy percent. First access takes three keys, and re-cut editions five. Keys can't be bought, and nothing is ever marked down.
- **dlive** — Quantities are stated up front. No countdown, and no restock. Tap a piece to see its story.
- **piece** — Each piece shows its number, grade and fit notes. One key, plus thirty-nine ninety-five, and it's yours.
- **in** — You're in. And the person who brought it back is told it found its encore. That's the loop.
- **OUTRO** — Bring it back. Swipe. Climb. Unlock. That's Encore.
