placeholder illustration everywhere in the app. No code changes.

Search the reference on zara.com to find the product page, then save its main
photo under the file name on the left.

.jpg, .jpeg and .png all work. Portrait photos on a plain background look best
(they are cropped to fill a 4:5 tile). The ids are the "id" values under
"garments" in Resources/seed.json.

Product photos belong to their owner. Use them for the competition demo only.
